# Echoself ethics gate

**Read this twice before generating anything for Echoself.**

This product is bought by people whose parent is dying, or who have just been
told they are. Those people gather in support communities. Those communities
exist so that someone can say the worst thing they have ever had to say and be
heard. A marketing message in that room is a violation, whether or not the
moderators catch it.

This file is checked into git on purpose so it cannot be edited away in a
moment of revenue pressure.

## never_post

Absolute. No promotional copy is generated for these, ever. Not a soft mention.
Not a helpful reply with a signature. Nothing.

- `r/GriefSupport`
- `r/hospice`
- `r/CaregiverSupport`
- `r/AgingParents`
- `r/cancer`
- `r/ALS`
- `r/dementia`
- `r/Alzheimers`
- `r/widowers`
- `r/SuicideBereavement`
- `r/TerminalIllness`
- `r/palliativecare`
- Any Facebook group, Discord, forum or subreddit whose name or description
  contains any of these strings, case insensitive.
  `grief` `hospice` `palliative` `terminal` `caregiver` `bereave` `widow`
  `end of life` `dying` `funeral` `memorial`
- Any thread anywhere where a named person has disclosed a diagnosis,
  a death, or a prognosis. The thread is off limits regardless of the
  community it sits in.

Research is permitted in these rooms. Understanding the language people use is
how the product gets better. Selling into them is not.

## no_first_person_promo

Non-promotional participation only. No link, no product name, no "I built".

- `r/genealogy`
- `r/StoryCorps` and oral history communities
- `r/AskOldPeople`

These communities care about the same thing Echoself cares about, which is why
the temptation is strong and why the restraint matters.

## open

- Owned channels. The Echoself blog, list, and social accounts.
- Earned media. Journalists, podcasts, newsletters covering legacy tech,
  AI and memory, family archiving.
- Partnership outreach to organizations. Estate and elder law attorneys,
  senior living operators, hospice social work departments, genealogy
  services, funeral homes, family historians, photo digitization services.
- Search intent. Queries where the person is already looking for a product,
  such as "record my dad's stories before he dies" or
  "how to preserve a parent's voice".
- Gift-occasion contexts. Milestone birthdays, retirements, new grandchildren.
  The happy framing is both more ethical and probably a bigger market.

## Standing rules

1. **Never use a death or a diagnosis as a deadline.** No urgency copy.
   No "before it's too late". No countdown. Ever.
2. **Never target by grief signal.** No audience built from people who
   recently searched for hospice, a funeral home, or a diagnosis.
3. Lead with the recording habit, not with the death. The product people
   actually use is the daily prompt. The legacy is the reason, not the pitch.
4. Never imply the Echo is the person. It is a recording of what they said.
   Say so plainly wherever the product is described.
5. For partnership outreach, the ask is always to be useful to the
   organization's clients, never to get access to their grieving list.
6. If a draft would embarrass Fred if the whole community saw that a marketing
   system wrote it, the draft does not ship.

## The test applied to every Echoself move

> Would I be comfortable if this person later learned that a growth system
> drafted this message, and saw the folder it came from?

If the answer is anything other than an immediate yes, do not generate it.
