## Note on offer 2

A printable or emailed question list is the right top of funnel for this
product. It is useful on its own with no account, it is shareable between
siblings, it seeds the exact habit the product depends on, and it carries none
of the ethical weight of a product pitch. Build this before any paid channel.

## Note on partnerships

Partnership outreach uses a different ask entirely. The offer to an estate
attorney or a senior living operator is a resource for their clients, not a
referral fee. Anything that looks like buying access to a grieving list is out.
