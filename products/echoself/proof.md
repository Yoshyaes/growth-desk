# Echoself proof

Verbatim only. Everything here comes from myechoself.com unless marked otherwise.

## Product facts

- [verified] Headline. "EchoSelf. Your Voice, Preserved Forever."
- [verified] Daily guided voice or text recording prompts.
- [verified] An AI Echo trained on the person's own memories and perspective.
- [verified] Selective access controls. The recorder chooses who can interact.
- [verified] Optional voice cloning, so the Echo replies in the person's own voice.
- [verified] A live demo Echo is playable without signing up.
- [verified] Pricing. Echo, text based, $9 a month. Echo Voice, with the voice
  clone, $19 a month. A free trial exists.

## Proof gaps, ranked

1. **No customer proof of any kind.** No quotes, no numbers, no stories.
   For a product bought on trust, this is the binding constraint.
2. **No plain statement of what happens to the recordings.** Where they are
   stored, who can reach them, what happens if Echoself shuts down, and what
   happens to the voice clone. A buyer handing over a parent's voice needs this
   answered before anything else, and it is not on the site.
3. **No retention evidence.** The whole product depends on a daily habit and
   there is no data showing the habit holds.

Closing gap 2 is worth more than any channel work. It is also the honest
thing to publish.
