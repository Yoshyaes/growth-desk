# Echoself voice

Register. Warm, plain, unhurried. Never urgent. Never sales-y.
Short sentences. No exclamation marks. No emoji.

Read `shared/voice-rules.md` and `ethics.md` first. Both govern.

## Sentences that sound right

- [verified, from the site] "Your Voice, Preserved Forever."
- [seed] "Five minutes a day. One question. Your own voice."
- [seed] "Nobody regrets having the tapes."
- [seed] "It is not your dad. It is what your dad said, in the order he said it."
- [seed] "Start with his eightieth birthday. Not with the hard part."

## Never say

- Any urgency framing. No "before it's too late". No countdown. No deadline
  built on someone's health.
- Anything implying the Echo is the person, is alive, or is a continuation.
- "Digital immortality" or any variant.
- Anything that positions the product against grief.

## Rules

- Lead with the habit. The daily prompt is what people use.
- The demo does the selling. Point at it and stop talking.
- When someone shares a loss, respond as a person and offer nothing.
