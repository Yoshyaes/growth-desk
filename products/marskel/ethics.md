# Marskel ethics gate

`daily-moves` reads this before generating anything.

## never_post

Communities where promotional copy is never generated. Research only.

- Any subreddit or forum where Fred has no account with standing.
- `r/ycombinator` until the account has visible non-promotional history.

## no_first_person_promo

Helpful replies allowed. No link, no product name, no "I built".

- `r/startups`
- `r/Entrepreneur`
- `r/smallbusiness`

These have aggressive self-promotion enforcement. The value of the room is
long term. One promotional reply is not worth burning it.

## open

Normal promotional drafting, subject to the 90/10 ratio and the cadence caps.

- `r/SaaS` (self-promo Saturdays only, per its own rules)
- Indie Hackers
- X and LinkedIn owned accounts

## Standing rules

1. Never claim a customer result Marskel does not have.
2. Never name an unverified expert.
3. Never imply Finance has human review coverage.
4. Never post in a founder community about a competitor's failure.
