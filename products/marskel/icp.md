# Marskel ICP

Every claim carries a tag. `verified` means evidenced. `assumed` means it is a
hypothesis and belongs in the test backlog. `dead` means it was tested and failed.

## Who buys

- [verified] Pre-seed to seed stage technical solo founders.
- [assumed] They have shipped product but have never run a go-to-market motion.
- [assumed] They have between $0 and $500k raised, and no budget for a
  fractional exec at $4k to $8k a month.
- [assumed] They are already using Claude Code or Cursor daily, so an AI-run
  function is not a conceptual leap for them.

## The trigger event that makes them buy today

- [assumed] An investor or advisor just told them to "get a fractional CMO"
  or "hire a head of sales", and they priced it and could not afford it.
- [assumed] They have a fundraise, a launch, or a board update in under six
  weeks and no one to build the material.
- [assumed] They tried a generic AI agent product, got a generic deliverable,
  and do not trust the category anymore.

## The top objection

- [verified] This category has a documented trust problem. Buyers do not
  believe AI-generated executive work is reliable enough to act on.
- [verified] The answer is the Red / Yellow / Green graduated autonomy model
  with named human expert reviewers approving deliverables. This must lead the
  marketing experience rather than sit buried on a features page.

## Who this is not for

- Non-technical founders. The product assumes they can judge the work.
- Funded teams with real hires. They buy people, not agents.
- Agencies looking to resell. Different product, different pricing.
