# Marskel voice

Register. Peer to peer with a technical founder. Concrete numbers.
Admit what is not built yet. Never oversell the autonomy.

Read `shared/voice-rules.md` first. It governs.

## Sentences that sound right

These are the reference set. Add to this file whenever Fred rewrites a draft
by more than half, and note what changed.

- [seed] "Ten AI agents, each backed by a named human who signs off before
  anything reaches you."
- [seed] "Green means the agent ships it. Yellow means a human reads it first.
  Red means a human writes it. You pick the line."
- [seed] "The reviewers are real people with real names and real track records.
  Adam Wall was Anthropic's first RevOps hire. He reviews the sales work."
- [seed] "Most AI exec products fail on trust, not on output quality.
  We built the review layer first and the agents second."

## Never say

- "AI executive team" as the lead. It is accurate and it converts badly,
  since it sounds like every other product in the category.
- Anything implying full autonomy. The human review layer is the product.
- Any claim about time saved or revenue generated. No evidence exists yet.
